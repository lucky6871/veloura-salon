# Veloura Salon — Design Direction

## Three possible directions

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Atelier Aura** | A warm editorial salon journal with layered photography, tailored typography, and quiet confidence. It feels like a private appointment with a fashion editor. | 0.07 |
| **Soft Modernism** | A serene, architectural approach built from ivory planes, fine charcoal lines, and tonal product-like composition. | 0.04 |
| **Midnight Gloss** | A dramatic after-hours beauty concept using lacquered dark surfaces and cinematic pools of light. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach: Atelier Aura

### Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial minimalism**, informed by independent beauty magazines, restrained Japanese hospitality, and the tactile calm of a luxury hotel spa.

### Core Principles

1. **Quietly directional:** every section uses a clear horizontal or vertical axis rather than a generic centered-card grid.
2. **Tactile restraint:** warm paper-like surfaces, hairline rules, cropped images, and small caps cues create material richness without visual clutter.
3. **Conversion through clarity:** the next useful action is obvious at all times, especially booking, contact, and directions.
4. **Editorial pacing:** generous whitespace alternates with intimate photography to make the scroll feel considered, not sales-driven.

### Color Philosophy

Ivory is the salon's breathing room; ink-charcoal gives every word weight; oat and soft champagne introduce warmth; **deep garnet** is reserved for decisive actions and selected moments of emphasis. The contrast stays crisp and composed rather than loud.

### Layout Paradigm

The page behaves like an unfolding salon folio: a split hero opens into an offset booking strip, service lists read as a tailored menu, and gallery images sit in a deliberately irregular editorial mosaic. Sections change pace instead of repeating identical boxes.

### Signature Elements

- Thin charcoal rules with small caps section labels.
- A vertical wordmark / monogram treatment that frames key transitions.
- Garnet circular seals for moments of recommendation and appointment action.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions feel like touching a quality appointment card: buttons compress slightly, underlines travel with purpose, and service rows reveal a direct booking cue. No playful or ornamental interaction competes with the user's intent.

### Animation

Use swift 180–280ms opacity and transform transitions with a deliberate ease-out. Photographs softly reveal upward as they enter the viewport; service rows shift only a few pixels on hover. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing entrance movement.

### Typography System

**Cormorant Garamond** gives display headings a high-contrast editorial character; **DM Sans** keeps navigation, prices, form labels, and body copy precise. Headlines use dramatic serif scale with tight tracking; utility labels use uppercase DM Sans with generous letter spacing.

### Brand Essence

**Veloura is an appointment-led beauty studio for people who value considered expertise, polished results, and unhurried care.**

Personality: **cultivated, warm, assured**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are evocative but specific; CTAs are direct and human; microcopy is calm and practical. Avoid generic welcome language and urgency theater.

> “A look that feels like your own — only more refined.”
>
> “Reserve the time your next look deserves.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark uses a considered serif “Veloura” with a compact, sculptural **V** monogram: two tapered strokes that meet like a folded ribbon or a strand of hair. The monogram works in a circle for the header, favicon, appointment seal, and footer.

### Signature Brand Color

**Veloura Garnet — #6F2C3A**

## Style Decisions

- No fabricated reviews or ratings. The review area is framed as a replaceable verified-review module until real testimonials are provided.
- Placeholder operational details (address, phone, WhatsApp) are intentionally labelled so the salon can replace them before publishing.

## Visual Refresh: Verde Nocturne

The refreshed direction replaces the delicate ivory-and-garnet dominance with a more recognisable **deep verdigris, warm porcelain, muted apricot, and sunlit saffron** story. Verdigris will create elegant moments of depth, apricot will make the service and editorial panels feel alive, and saffron will guide decisive actions without reading as a generic beauty pink. The page will gain more rhythm through layered colour planes, circular seals, framed image captions, and intentional contrast shifts, while the booking path remains immediate and calm.

## Navy Luxe Redesign

### Design Movement

**Modern beauty futurism** with the cinematic restraint of a luxury fragrance film: deep midnight spaces, translucent lavender panes, precise rose-gold details, and deliberate voids for editorial typography.

### Core Principles

1. **Immersive depth:** stacked transparent planes, soft blur, and refracted colour create a sense of a rich physical environment.
2. **Refined contrast:** white display type blooms out of deep navy; rose gold is used as a jewellery-like accent, never a dominant fill.
3. **Motion with purpose:** simulated hero-video movement, controlled parallax, light sweeps, and text reveals support the scroll instead of distracting from it.
4. **Beauty as architecture:** service, gallery, and location sections use strong framing, visual cadence, and image scale rather than repeated conventional cards.

### Color Philosophy

**Midnight navy** carries the brand's authority and sophistication. **Soft lavender** provides a memorable atmospheric glow. **Pearl white** makes reading effortless. **Rose gold** signals touchpoints, premium craftsmanship, and appointment actions.

### Signature Elements

- Translucent lavender glass panels with fine rose-gold borders.
- A moving light-orbit and elegant lens flare behind hero content.
- Tall, cropped photography framed as a luxury beauty campaign.

### Motion System

The hero uses a cinematic static image with a subtle Ken Burns push, ambient lavender bloom, and a moving light sweep as the video fallback. Section content reveals by translate-and-fade on intersection, while images have shallow parallax offsets and hover depth. Motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Style Decisions

- **Navy Luxe Redesign** is the active visual direction and supersedes Atelier Aura and Verde Nocturne for palette, atmosphere, materials, and imagery.
- Rose gold is reserved as a jewellery-like accent for borders, seals, icons, and appointment cues rather than broad background colour.
- Copy stays cinematic, assured, and specific; it does not use generic salon welcomes or urgency-driven booking language.
